## Problem being solved

Koha can record circulation, but it does not natively answer whether library promotion work is producing measurable use of the promoted collection. A campaign record such as “154 titles displayed” is operationally useful but insufficient for management because it does not immediately answer:

1. How many promoted titles were actually borrowed?
2. How many checkout transactions occurred during the campaign?
3. What share of the promoted collection was used?
4. Did circulation increase compared with the immediately preceding comparable period?
5. Which titles improved, which produced no response, and which generated repeat demand?
6. Is current demand strong enough to justify collection-development review?
7. Which campaign dimensions perform better when attribution is defensible?

v0.5 changes the plugin from an activity-count dashboard into an evidence-oriented promotion analytics system.

## Core analytics hierarchy

Every major analytics-bearing surface follows four questions:

- **Reach:** What did the library promote?
- **Engagement:** What was actually borrowed?
- **Impact:** How did use compare with the equal baseline period?
- **Action:** What should staff investigate or do next?

Workflow counters such as holds, approvals and “Sent to Koha” remain useful, but they are secondary to engagement evidence.

## Active-campaign correction

### Previous defect

An active campaign with no `end_date` used its `start_date` as the effective end. This could reduce a long-running active campaign to a one-day measurement window and made staff feel they had to complete the campaign before useful analytics appeared.

### v0.5 rule

For a started campaign with `status = active`:

- no configured end date: effective end = the current/as-of date;
- configured future end date: effective end = the current/as-of date;
- configured past end date: effective end = the configured end date and a data-quality warning is returned because status is still active;
- campaign start in the future: the campaign remains scheduled/pending.

The Baseline period always has the same calendar-day duration as the resolved During period.

Active-campaign results are marked **live-to-date / provisional**. Staff never need to falsely complete an active campaign merely to calculate current performance.

## Follow-up-window states

After 7/14/30/60 windows now expose explicit states:

- **Pending:** the window has not begun; metrics are null and the UI displays “Pending”, never zero.
- **To date / partial:** the window has started but is not complete.
- **Complete:** the full window is available.

A future follow-up period must never be presented as a zero-response result.

## Titles versus copies/items

Koha circulation events are item-based, while management impact is often title-based.

v0.5 therefore distinguishes:

- **Promoted copies/items:** distinct Koha `itemnumber` values linked to the campaign.
- **Promoted titles:** distinct Koha `biblionumber` values represented by those item links.

This explains legitimate cases such as 156 linked copies/items representing 154 distinct titles. Title utilization is calculated at bibliographic-title level so multiple copies do not inflate the breadth-of-use measure.

## KPI definitions

### Promoted titles

Distinct `biblionumber` values represented by eligible campaign item links.

### Promoted copies/items

Distinct eligible `itemnumber` values.

### Titles used

Distinct promoted titles with one or more checkout events during the resolved During period.

### Title utilization rate

`titles_used / promoted_titles × 100`

This is the primary breadth-of-engagement measure.

### Campaign checkouts

De-duplicated Koha checkout events for eligible promoted items during the resolved During period.

### Baseline checkouts

Checkout events for the same eligible promoted-item cohort during the immediately preceding equal-duration Baseline period.

### Daily checkout rate

`checkout_count / calendar_days_in_window`

### Circulation-rate change / uplift

`(during_daily_rate - baseline_daily_rate) / baseline_daily_rate × 100`

When baseline rate is zero, percentage uplift remains null rather than reporting infinity. The UI may describe a positive during-period result as **New response**.

### Increased-use titles

Distinct promoted titles where During checkout count is greater than Baseline checkout count.

### Zero-response titles

Distinct promoted titles with zero checkout events during the resolved During period.

For a campaign that has not started, this value is null/pending rather than falsely equal to all promoted titles.

### Repeat-demand titles

Distinct promoted titles with at least two checkout events during the resolved During period.

### Days to first checkout

Elapsed days from campaign start to the first qualifying checkout; null when no qualifying event exists.

## Page architecture

### Dashboard

Primary cards now communicate:

- Promoted titles;
- Titles used;
- Title utilization;
- Campaign-period checkouts with baseline context;
- Zero-response titles;
- Active campaigns.

Campaign/completion/item/location counts remain available as portfolio-administration context rather than dominating the first view.

Recent Promotions now includes title use, checkouts, utilization and baseline change so staff can compare campaigns without opening every record.

### Promotions

The list now exposes:

- promoted titles and linked copy/item count;
- titles used;
- campaign checkouts;
- utilization;
- change versus baseline;
- active live-to-date status.

### Campaign Detail

An **Impact at a glance** panel presents title reach, title use, utilization, checkout volume, baseline comparison and zero-response count, with direct links to full Campaign Analytics and Resource Impact.

### Campaign Analytics

The primary hierarchy is:

1. promoted titles / linked copies;
2. titles used;
3. title utilization;
4. campaign checkouts;
5. baseline checkouts;
6. daily-rate change versus baseline.

Response signals then show increased-use, zero-response and repeat-demand titles. Measurement windows show Pending / To date / Complete states. The title-response table aggregates multiple copies by `biblionumber`.

### Promoted Resource Impact

The former Book Display Impact UI is generalized to **Promoted Resource Impact** because email, recommendation and digital campaigns can also promote Koha titles.

The first row now answers whether the promoted resources were used:

- Promoted titles;
- Titles borrowed;
- Title utilization;
- Campaign checkouts with baseline;
- Increased-use titles;
- Zero-response titles.

Collection-development workflow remains in a secondary section:

- High priority;
- Active holds;
- Approved;
- Sent to Koha.

The internal route `book_display_impact` and native Koha Suggestion reason `Book Display Impact` remain stable for backward compatibility.

### Comparative Reports

Portfolio headline metrics now lead with campaigns measured, promoted titles, titles used, title utilization, campaign checkouts and baseline checkouts.

Multi-attribution is retained under **Measurement quality** rather than presented as an engagement KPI.

Dimension tables (channel, location, campaign type, language, audience) now include promoted titles, titles used, utilization, baseline checkouts, campaign checkouts, checkout change, zero-response titles and increased-use titles.

Location conclusions remain conservative: multi-location campaigns can contribute to the comparison table but cannot prove which physical location caused a checkout. “Leading location” evidence only uses campaigns assigned to one real configured location and excludes `unassigned`.

## Portfolio de-duplication

Overlapping campaigns may legitimately qualify for the same checkout. Per-campaign analytics can multi-attribute the event, but portfolio totals de-duplicate by Koha `issue_id`.

Promoted titles and promoted items are also de-duplicated across the selected portfolio scope.

## Responsible interpretation

The plugin measures circulation response. It may state that:

- circulation increased during a promotion;
- a larger share of promoted titles was used;
- a title generated increased or repeat demand.

It must not claim that a promotion directly caused every checkout or that a physical display was definitely seen by a specific borrower.

No patron-identifiable analytics are exposed on these management screens.

## Koha 26.05 compatibility status

Code/dependency review remains favorable, but runtime compatibility is **not yet claimed**.

A fresh 26.05 KTD image pull was attempted again on 2026-09-21 after Windows C: had approximately 16 GB free. During layer expansion free space fell to approximately 12 GB and then 11 GB before the image completed. The pull was stopped deliberately to protect the workstation. No `promoeng2605` containers and no completed `koha/koha-testing:26.05` image were created.

This proves that the former 12–15 GB free-space threshold was insufficient on this host. Do not retry until at least **20–25 GB of safe Windows C: free space** is available.

No global Docker pruning or unrelated-container cleanup is authorized as a workaround.
