# KohaCon26 Demonstration and Video Package

KohaCon26 takes place at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany,
5–9 October 2026. The plugin should be presented as an evidence-led Koha
extension and evaluation release, not as an already production-certified product.

## Core conference story

1. Libraries invest staff time in displays and promotion.
2. Ordinary circulation reports do not retain what was displayed, where or when.
3. The plugin records that context while Koha remains the source of truth.
4. Equal before/during/after windows turn a display into measurable evidence.
5. Book Display Impact connects successful promotion to unmet demand.
6. Librarians retain judgment; approved recommendations enter native Koha Suggestions.
7. Acquisitions staff retain vendor, fund, price and basket authority.

Suggested demonstration title:
**From Book Display to Collection Decision: Evidence-Led Promotion in Koha**

## Eight-minute live demonstration

- 0:00–0:45 — State the problem and safeguards.
- 0:45–1:30 — Show the academic dashboard.
- 1:30–2:30 — Open campaign 40 and its three display locations.
- 2:30–3:45 — Show before/during/after Analytics and title response.
- 3:45–5:30 — Open Book Display Impact and explain holds/copy pressure.
- 5:30–6:30 — Approve two copies and send to Koha Suggestions.
- 6:30–7:15 — Open the native Koha suggestion.
- 7:15–8:00 — Explain privacy, limitations and public roadmap.
## Installation video script

### Opening

“This video installs Koha Promotion & Engagement as a normal KPZ plugin.
Use staging first and take a database backup before beginning.”

### Recording sequence

1. Show the GitHub Release page and version number.
2. Download the KPZ and checksum.
3. Demonstrate checksum verification.
4. Sign in to the Koha staff interface.
5. Open Administration, then Manage plugins.
6. Choose Upload plugin and select the KPZ.
7. Confirm the upload and installation.
8. Show version 0.4.0 with no plugin load errors.
9. Run the tool and open Configuration.
10. Add one sample location.
11. Create one sample campaign with a valid barcode.
12. Open Campaign Detail, Analytics and Book Display Impact.
13. Explain Suggestions permission before demonstrating submission.
14. Show where troubleshooting and rollback are documented.

Narration must explain what success looks like after every click and what to do
when the expected control is missing.
## User video script

1. Create a promotion and explain each field.
2. Paste or scan barcodes and explain all-or-nothing validation.
3. Show multiple reusable locations.
4. Save and open the campaign detail.
5. Show audit history and linked Koha items.
6. Open Analytics and explain comparable windows.
7. Open Book Display Impact and explain every column.
8. Approve a recommendation.
9. Submit it to Koha Suggestions.
10. Show the acquisition officer's native Koha workflow.
11. Show comparative Reports and CSV/JSON exports.
12. Close with privacy and causation limitations.

## Recording standards

- Record at 1920×1080 with browser zoom at 100%.
- Use a clean test database with invented patrons and campaign data.
- Never display credentials, patron names or production URLs.
- Keep the pointer visible and pause after each click.
- Add chapter markers and English captions.
- Prepare an offline MP4 and screenshots in case the live network fails.
- Include the exact release tag and checksum in the video description.

## Conference readiness gate

Before presenting, re-run all automated tests, exact-KPZ clean installation,
Koha 26.05 compatibility and a full rehearsal. Keep the KPZ, checksum, SOP,
screenshots and offline video on two separate devices.
